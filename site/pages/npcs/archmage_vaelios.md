# Archmage Vaelios

Archmage Vaelios is the unchallenged master of the Arcane Sanctum, a figure of ageless wisdom and barely restrained power. Though centuries old, his silver-white hair remains unblemished by time, and his deep violet eyes gleam with secrets few dare to comprehend. He moves with effortless grace, his indigo robes shifting like liquid starlight, embroidered with constellations that flicker and change. Vaelios detests politics but understands its necessity, begrudgingly engaging with Queen Lysara and Chamberlain Auron when required. He sees Magister Halvar Greythorn as a well-meaning fool, a man too rigid in his understanding of law to see the true complexities of power. His true rivalry lies with Royal Archivist Edric Kael—the two engage in long-winded philosophical debates, often arguing whether history should be remembered as it was or as it was intended to be. Though he dismisses many as beneath his intellect, he keeps a wary eye on Selene Varro, knowing that spies often have ways of uncovering knowledge best left forgotten. Vaelios also avoids Old Man Griggs, feigning disinterest but showing visible discomfort when the so-called "drunken prophet" lingers near the sanctum. He suspects Griggs is something unnatural, though he lacks the proof to confirm his suspicions.

## Notes
Master of the Arcane Sanctum, wise and powerful with a deep knowledge of magic and politics.
