# Brother Alden Raye

A human male, Brother Alden Raye is the youngest and most hopeful among the clergy, his faith rooted in mercy, redemption, and unwavering belief in the good of people. With soft brown hair and kind hazel eyes, he lacks the imposing presence of his fellow clergy, but his conviction is ironclad.

Alden clashes constantly with Sir Roderic Vaughn, believing mercy saves more souls than steel ever could. He admires Sister Myra Fenwick, who shares his desire to help others, though her methods are far more pragmatic. Father Cassiel guides him like a mentor, tempering his optimism with hard truths, though Alden often wrestles with the idea that not everyone can be saved.

Though he avoids violence when possible, Alden is no coward—when he takes a stand, he does not yield.

## Notes
A young, idealistic member of the clergy who believes in mercy above all.
