# Captain Darian Veldrin

Captain Darian Veldrin is the sword and shield of Valeris, a hardened soldier turned commander of the Iron Guard. He is not a man of flowery words or courtly games—his authority comes from experience, discipline, and a reputation for never backing down. With close-cropped black hair, a battle-worn face, and a towering presence, he ensures that the palace remains untouchable and that the Queen’s enemies do not live long enough to become threats. Darian’s loyalty is to Valeris first and to the Queen second, though he would never admit it. He despises the political scheming of the nobility and holds a particular disdain for Chamberlain Auron, seeing him as a man who plays games with lives while Darian sheds blood to protect the kingdom. His uneasy relationship with Selene Varro, the Master of Whispers, is one of necessity rather than trust—her intelligence is useful, but he considers her work dishonorable. He barely tolerates Malrik Dunholm, the prison warden whose cruelty he sees as excessive, but he recognizes the necessity of maintaining order. He has crossed paths with Kaela Voss more than once, the bounty hunter operating outside the law but often cleaning up the messes that politics create. Though he follows orders without question, there are whispers in the court that should the Queen ever falter, it would not be an outside enemy that challenges her rule, but the man who commands her guard.

## Notes
A pragmatic ruler, skilled in politics and strategy, balancing power through manipulation and force.
