# Captain Rhogar Skyscale

Captain Rhogar Skyscale is a bronze dragonborn with a sharp grin, a sharper sword, and the sharpest wit in Valeris. Once a proud naval officer, Rhogar now sails the skies as the captain of the Storm Wraith, a skyship that answers to no kingdom. With gilded bronze scales that shimmer under torchlight and a long scar across his snout, he is both charismatic and dangerous, a man who plays by his own rules.

Once a hero of the Valerian Navy, Rhogar’s reckless tactics and defiance of orders led to his dishonorable discharge. Rather than lick his wounds, he turned to smuggling, sky piracy, and the occasional mercenary contract, running goods and secrets across the continent. He still has connections within the military, though General Garric Thornvale would rather see him hanged than pardoned.

Rhogar has strong ties to Wilfred "Willy" Greaves, often employing the halfling scout for jobs that require subtlety. He also has dealings with Rivak the Hollow, though their partnership is tense at best—Rhogar doesn’t trust magic, and Rivak doesn’t trust anyone.

Despite his outlaw reputation, Rhogar isn’t a villain. He has his own sense of honor, helping those in need when it suits him. But at the end of the day, he is a man of the sky, bound to nothing but the wind and the weight of his own legend.
