# Dain Emberforge

Dain Emberforge is a broad-shouldered, grizzled dwarf whose very presence commands respect. His deep red beard is often dusted with soot, and his arms, thick as forge hammers, bear the burns and scars of decades spent crafting weapons for the warriors of Valeris. As master blacksmith of the Iron Oath Armory, he runs the forge with unwavering discipline, ensuring that every blade, breastplate, and battleaxe that leaves his shop is worthy of legend. Dain is blunt, impatient with incompetence, and has little time for idle chatter—his focus is steel, and steel alone.

He has a begrudging respect for Garrek Steelson, whom he sees as a worthy successor if the man ever stops being so damn stubborn. While he constantly scolds Vella Dornfire for rushing her work, he secretly admires her drive and innovation, though he’d sooner die than admit it outright. Rivak the Hollow, however, is a necessary evil. Dain doesn’t trust the tiefling, nor does he like where some of the rare materials come from, but quality steel requires quality goods, and Rivak delivers. If the supplier ever crosses a line, though, Dain won’t hesitate to personally introduce him to his own anvil.

Though he avoids city politics, Dain has long provided weapons to the Valeris military, and he still has contacts within General Garric Thornvale’s ranks. He refuses to work with criminals—at least, not openly—but he’s aware that some of his best work has found its way into the hands of men like Durnik "The Scar" Vale. As long as they pay and don’t use his steel for cowardly deeds, Dain keeps his forge burning, his hands steady, and his eyes open.

## Notes
