# Durnik "The Scar" Vale

Durnik "The Scar" Vale is the weathered and calculating human owner of The Broken Dagger. His scarred face and watchful eyes betray a past steeped in violence, though these days, he’s more concerned with maintaining control over the seediest bar in Valeris than wielding a blade himself. A former mercenary, he now acts as a neutral broker for thieves, assassins, and those looking to deal in secrets. He has an unspoken truce with Selene Varro, who allows his establishment to exist so long as he feeds her network information when needed. He keeps Silas Crowne close, though he doesn’t trust him, knowing the rogue plays too many sides. His most pressing concern is Lazari "Rat" Fenwick, the goblin snitch who constantly skitters around, selling secrets to whoever pays—Durnik hasn’t decided yet whether Rat is more useful alive or dead.
