# Eldrin Starweave

Eldrin Starweave is the enigmatic proprietor of The Owl and Ember, a shop teeming with magical oddities, arcane tomes, and potions of dubious potency. Tall and slender, with silver-streaked midnight blue hair and piercing violet eyes, he carries himself with the air of a man lost in his own thoughts—because he usually is. His deep indigo robes shimmer faintly, embroidered with celestial symbols that seem to move if you stare too long.

A scholar first, a businessman second, and a conversationalist last, Eldrin often forgets what he’s saying mid-sentence, only to remember hours later and resume the conversation as if no time had passed. He is a vast well of arcane knowledge, though getting him to share it in a coherent manner is a test of patience. He is endlessly frustrated by his familiar, Nyx, a mischievous shadowy owl-like creature that delights in disorganizing his shop just to watch him struggle.

Eldrin has an uneasy but necessary relationship with Archmage Vaelios, the only mage in Valeris whose knowledge might rival his own. He respects the Sanctum’s authority but chafes at their restrictions on magic. He often provides rare components to Sister Myra Fenwick, though he insists on debating the nature of divine magic with her every time she visits. His dealings with Rivak the Hollow are purely transactional—he does not trust the tiefling’s sources, but some materials are too rare to question.

Despite his absentmindedness, Eldrin is not to be underestimated. Beneath the eccentricities lies a mage of considerable power, and those who think to take advantage of him often find themselves regretting it—if they even remember what happened afterward.
