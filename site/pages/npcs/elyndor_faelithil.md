# Elyndor Faelithil

## Notes
Wise and contemplative, Elyndor often prefers observation over action but is decisive when necessary. He is a staunch believer in balance and the protection of nature.

## Appearance
A striking high elf with silvery hair that shimmers like moonlight and glowing violet eyes. His elegant robes of green and gold are inscribed with glowing runes.

## Class
Wizard (School of Abjuration) / Fighter (Eldritch Knight)
