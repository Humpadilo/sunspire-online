# Elyssa Dorne

Elyssa Dorne is a human noblewoman of wealth and mystery, known both for her generosity and the whispered rumors that follow her every move. With dark chestnut hair elegantly braided, and piercing blue-gray eyes that see too much, she is an enigma wrapped in silken dresses and carefully measured smiles. Though she plays the part of a gracious philanthropist, those who watch closely know she moves like a strategist playing a game no one else can see.

Elyssa funds hospitals, orphanages, and public projects, always presenting herself as a noblewoman who cares for the people. Yet, beneath that kindness, she is watching, listening, waiting. Father Cassiel Dornhelm appreciates her financial support but questions her motives, wondering if her generosity is true faith or calculated investment. Selene Varro keeps an eye on her as well, suspecting Elyssa of backing secret causes that could shift power within Valeris.

Despite her reputation among the nobility, Elyssa is known to venture into the slums, though no one quite knows why. Some whisper that she is involved with criminal elements, while others believe she is simply gathering secrets more valuable than gold. What is certain is that Elyssa Dorne is not simply a noblewoman playing charity—she is a force moving in the shadows, and one day, the kingdom will feel the weight of whatever she is planning.
