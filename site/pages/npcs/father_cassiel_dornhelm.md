# Father Cassiel Dornhelm

A human male, Father Cassiel Dornhelm is the unshakable leader of the Valeris Church, a man whose wisdom carries as much weight as his faith. His silver-streaked black hair and lined, weathered face tell of decades spent in service, carrying the burdens of others while rarely voicing his own.

Cassiel seeks balance in all things, often caught between Brother Alden Raye’s idealism and Sir Roderic Vaughn’s severity. He respects Alden’s mercy but knows it alone cannot protect the people. He values Roderic’s strength but warns against the dangers of a heart hardened beyond reason. He relies on Sister Myra Fenwick’s keen insight, often trusting her instincts when it comes to uncovering corruption within the church or nobility.

Though Cassiel’s patience is vast, it is not endless. When pushed too far, his voice alone can silence even the loudest room.
