# Garrek Steelson

Garrek Steelson is built like a war machine, his muscled frame a testament to years spent on the battlefield before an injury forced him to trade his sword for a hammer. His sharp brown eyes, streaked with the weariness of a soldier-turned-smith, see every imperfection in the steel he works. Once a decorated fighter in the Valeris military, Garrek now serves as Dain Emberforge’s right hand at the Iron Oath Armory, using his firsthand experience with weapons to craft some of the finest blades in the kingdom.

Garrek butts heads with Dain constantly—where the old dwarf values tradition, Garrek believes in adaptability. Their arguments echo through the forge, but neither would trade the other for a lesser craftsman. He has a complicated relationship with Vella Dornfire, seeing her as reckless yet undeniably talented, and their constant competitions over who can forge the better blade have become something of a spectacle among the apprentices.

If there’s one man Garrek doesn’t trust, it’s Rivak the Hollow. He hates dealing with the tiefling supplier, convinced that one day Rivak’s "business" will bring trouble to the forge. He’s also wary of General Thornvale, having lost friends to the man's uncompromising commands during his military days. Despite this, he remains loyal to Valeris—just in his own way, with hammer and steel rather than rank and orders.

## Notes
A former soldier who now forges weapons with precision and care.
