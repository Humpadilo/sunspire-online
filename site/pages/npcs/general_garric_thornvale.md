# General Garric Thornvale

A human male, General Garric Thornvale is the unwavering force behind Valeris’ military, a seasoned commander who has led men through war and lived to tell the tale. His broad, battle-scarred frame, streaks of silver in his dark hair, and piercing steel-blue eyes leave no doubt that he is a warrior first, a leader second, and a politician only when necessary.

Thornvale is ruthless on the battlefield but fair to his soldiers. He expects excellence, not perfection, and has no patience for cowardice or insubordination. While he respects Sergeant Braska Holt’s grit, he wishes she had more discipline. He finds Wilfred "Willy" Greaves insufferable, though begrudgingly admits the man’s skills are invaluable. His relationship with Prison Warden Malrik Dunholm is tense—Thornvale believes in military law, not tyranny, and sees Malrik’s heavy-handed approach as dangerous.

Despite his rigid nature, Thornvale cares for his soldiers like a father, though he will never admit it. His commands are absolute, but his goal is clear—Valeris must never fall.

## Notes
A battle-hardened leader, focused on protecting Valeris with unwavering authority.
