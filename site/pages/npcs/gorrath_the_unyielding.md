# Gorrath "The Unyielding"

## Notes
Grim, disciplined, and utterly resolute. Gorrath believes strength is earned through pain and endurance. He rarely speaks, but when he does, it’s either a battle cry or a warning. He is the immovable rock among a sea of chaos.

## Appearance
A hulking orc clad in ancient, battered armor engraved with battle scars. His crimson eyes glare beneath a heavy brow, and deep scars crisscross his face. His axe looks like it’s been cleaving skulls for decades.

## Class
Barbarian (Path of the Juggernaut)
