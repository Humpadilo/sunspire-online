# Grenak Goateater

Grenak hails from a lovely village settled on rolling hills with scattering boulders and sheep. And Grenak, being a handsome and strong orc, finds himself yearning for a bride. A beautiful lady orc. And Grenak has his eye on the most beautiful lady orc of all, Bertha. The largest, most voluptuous lady Orc in all the village. And nothing but the best will do for his intended bethrothed. In his village's tradition, it is customary to present a demon skull to your intended, the bigger the better. And as Bertha is the largest and most coveted of all the Orcs in Fergenstoren Village, Grenak must find the largest demon he could possibly identify and remove his head, dragging the skull back to his one and only bethrothed. So off he left, traveling the lands in search of the biggest demon he can find. 

During his journeys, he came across a handsome traveler named Reese, with a burning intent to kill demons. This is entertaining to Grenak and Reese's fighting skills impress him. Also, Reese enjoys a good drink, as does Grenak. Therefore Grenak has decided to travel with Reese, because there is nothing more that Grenak loves than killing demons and tavern brawling. 

And so their adventure begins.
