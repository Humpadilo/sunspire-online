# Grizzik the Embered

## Notes
Unhinged, unpredictable, and delightfully chaotic. Grizzik thrives on mayhem, laughs in the face of danger (and sometimes reality), and solves problems with fire... usually a lot of it. Despite her madness, she’s fiercely loyal to her companions in her own warped way.

## Appearance
A wiry goblin with jagged teeth and glowing orange eyes. Her patchwork robes are scorched and crackling with unstable energy. Trinkets, bones, and arcane charms hang from her ears and wrists, some still smoking from her last magical outburst.

## Class
Sorcerer (Wild Magic) / Warlock (The Fiend)
