# Kaela Voss

Kaela Voss is a half-orc bounty hunter whose reputation in Valeris is equal parts legend and cautionary tale. Tall and broad-shouldered, with dark green skin, piercing amber eyes, and scars that map out a history of hard-won fights, she carries herself with the confidence of someone who has never lost a hunt. Wrapped in a tattered but sturdy leather coat, with a pair of matched axes strapped to her back, Kaela is as much a storyteller as she is a hunter.

She has spent years tracking dangerous criminals, deserters, and fugitives across Sunspire, and when she’s not on a job, she can often be found in taverns like the Gilded Gryphon, swapping bounty tales for drinks. Despite her gruff and sarcastic demeanor, Kaela has a strong sense of justice—she hunts those who deserve it, not those with the fattest purses. She has a deep respect for General Garric Thornvale, having worked with the military on several high-profile captures, but has little patience for the likes of Prison Warden Malrik Dunholm, whom she considers needlessly cruel.

Kaela has a long and complicated history with Veyna "The Ghost" Kael, having once been contracted to capture her. The job failed—not because Kaela lacked skill, but because Veyna had been set up. Rather than turning her in, Kaela let her go, creating a silent understanding between them. While they haven’t crossed paths in years, they both know their next meeting could just as easily be a reunion—or a battle to the death.

For all her strength and skill, Kaela carries herself as someone who laughs in the face of death—not because she isn’t afraid of it, but because she knows one day, it will catch up to her. Until then, she intends to drink, fight, and tell as many stories as she can before the last hunt finally finds her.
