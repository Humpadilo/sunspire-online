# Kelsynk Tchotcke

Kelsynk is a resourceful artificer, using his knack for tinkering to craft gadgets and contraptions from the wreckage of the world around him.  He is interested in the things others toss aside—the scraps no one thinks twice about. A discarded spring, an old shard of glass, a broken tool… to most, it's trash; to him, it's potential. These are the bones of new inventions, the foundations of clever traps, or weapons that could turn the tide of battle. Each gadget a self-proclaimed work of intricate design, each one pushing the boundaries of goblin ingenuity. He does not just make things to blow up (though sometimes that was part of the fun), he makes things that solve problems, open doors, or uncover secrets hidden deep in the earth.

So, while others may chase after power, Kelsynk’s eyes are set on something far more valuable: the knowledge of the past, and the ingenuity of forgotten civilizations. And if he happens to collect a few shiny trinkets along the way, well... that's just the "prize" of doing business.

Kelsynk Tchotcke may not be the loudest or the flashiest, but in the ruins of the Deadlands, it’s the quiet ones who make off with the biggest prizes. He’ll keep taking, keep learning, until he’s uncovered every last secret buried beneath the earth.
