# Krivahn "The Verdant Fang"

## Notes
Stoic and deeply spiritual, Krivahn is a protector of nature and balance. She speaks only when necessary, but her words carry weight

## Appearance
A towering crocodilian Beastfolk with emerald-green scales and golden eyes. Her gear is crafted from the natural resources of Mireholm, adorned with charms and talismans.

## Class
Barbarian (Path of the Ancestral Guardian) / Druid (Circle of the Land - Swamp)
