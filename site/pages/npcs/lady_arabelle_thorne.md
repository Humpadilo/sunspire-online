# Lady Arabelle Thorne

## Notes
Pragmatic, decisive, and fiercely protective of her people. She has a stern demeanor but carries the weight of past decisions and the lives lost under her command during the infernal invasion.

## Appearance
A tall, commanding woman in her mid-40s with piercing blue eyes and streaks of silver in her dark hair. She wears a blend of practical armor and regal finery, symbolizing her dual role as a warrior and leader.

## Class
Paladin/Fighter
