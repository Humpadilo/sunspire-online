# Lazari "Rat" Fenwick

Lazari "Rat" Fenwick is a wiry, slippery goblin with twitchy fingers and an even twitchier mind. He thrives on secrets and the fear of being caught, selling information to whoever will pay, even if it means playing two sides of the same fight. While Durnik tolerates him for now, Selene Varro has personally warned Rat about selling information outside her network—he may have just enough sense to listen, but only time will tell. He’s terrified of Veyna "The Ghost" Kael, convinced she’s watching him even when she’s nowhere to be seen. The only person he trusts, if you could call it that, is Sniv the Unlucky, who he often meets in dark alleyways to swap gossip and gold. He and Sniv work together to smuggle contraband into the city, though Rat is careful not to cross Gerrold Dunn, who would have him locked up in Dunholm’s Hold without hesitation.

## Notes
A slippery goblin dealer of secrets, thriving on deception and danger.
