# Lina Farrow

Lina Farrow is the heart of the Gilded Gryphon, a lively, sharp-witted barmaid with shoulder-length auburn hair and hazel eyes that sparkle with mischief. She has a talent for making patrons feel welcome—unless she doesn’t like them, in which case they usually find themselves on the receiving end of a withering glare and a spilled drink. While Borin Thaldrum handles the business and Mara the kitchen, Lina keeps the social gears turning, knowing every regular by name and every scandal by heart. She has a soft spot for Sniv the Unlucky, often sneaking him food when Mara isn’t looking, though she makes sure he never cheats inside the inn. She and Bert Nolley work seamlessly together behind the bar, their banter keeping the long nights entertaining. While she enjoys the chaotic energy of the Gryphon, she knows when to call Garruk Stonefist if things start getting out of hand. Lina has a talent for gathering secrets, and while she’s not officially part of Selene Varro’s network, she’s been known to trade a particularly juicy piece of gossip when the price is right.
