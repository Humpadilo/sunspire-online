# Magister Halvar Greythorn

Magister Halvar Greythorn is a human magistrate whose presence in Valeris is as unyielding as the laws he enforces. A tall, severe-looking man with iron-gray hair slicked back in strict order and a posture that never slouches, Halvar embodies discipline and order. His sharp blue eyes are calculating, missing nothing, and his deep navy robes are always immaculate, a stark contrast to the filth and corruption he constantly fights to root out.

As the highest arbiter of law in Valeris, Halvar is feared by criminals and respected by the nobility. He detests the lawlessness of the Broken Dagger, seeing it as a festering wound on the city’s reputation. He has clashed with Durnik "The Scar" Vale many times, but Durnik remains just careful enough to avoid outright breaking the laws Halvar enforces.

Halvar maintains a tense alliance with Master of Whispers Selene Varro. Though he dislikes spies and their methods, he knows that information is often the sharpest blade in his arsenal. The two share a mutual understanding—Halvar keeps things legally clean, while Selene deals with what the law cannot touch. They have never fully trusted one another, but both acknowledge that Valeris would be worse off without their combined efforts.

Despite his cold and calculating nature, Halvar is not corrupt. He truly believes in justice, and though his methods are harsh, his rulings are always fair. His presence is a reminder that while the streets may be filled with crime and deception, the Queen’s law still holds power.
