# Malrik Dunholm

A dwarf male, Malrik Dunholm rules the Valeris prison with an iron fist—sometimes literally. With a thick, jet-black beard, a shaved head, and arms as solid as iron bars, he embodies the concept of law as absolute.

Malrik believes in punishment fitting the crime, though his idea of “fitting” is often extreme. The guilty suffer, the weak fall, and only the strong endure. He sees General Thornvale as too lenient, Braska Holt as too reckless, and Wilfred Greaves as a waste of a uniform. “Soldiers fight. Spies skulk. I know which I respect.”

Despite his harsh nature, Malrik is not corrupt. He truly believes he protects Valeris by keeping its criminals caged—he just has no sympathy for those who fail to meet his standard.

## Notes
