# Merribelle Fairburrow

## Notes
Lighthearted and clever, Merribelle often uses humor to diffuse tension. Beneath her cheerful demeanor lies a sharp mind and unshakable resolve.

## Appearance
A cheerful halfling with curly auburn hair and a freckled face. She wears practical clothing with a finely crafted leather vest adorned with small trinkets.

## Class
Rogue (Mastermind) / Bard (College of Lore)
