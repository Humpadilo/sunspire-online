# Mruno Bars

Comes from the Deadlands, rushing away as fast as he can. In hot pursuit is a band of misfits- The Mythic Golden Minotaur Grand Guild who seem to want a pound of my flesh... Saying I owe them money or something hahahaha...hahaha ha. I travel the world to spread my music and the legs of any damsel who will let me. Not much is known about me, but my fabled Musical Troupe- The Silken Sonic- is known far and wide for bringing the best music and entertainment to the lands. Without their forerunner, how will the band stay together?
