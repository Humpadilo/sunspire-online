# Nyx

Nyx is an infuriatingly clever and endlessly smug shadowy owl-like creature that serves as Eldrin Starweave’s familiar, though "serves" is a generous term. With sleek black feathers, glowing violet eyes, and a penchant for speaking in riddles, Nyx delights in making Eldrin’s life as difficult as possible. He knocks over potion bottles, swaps labels on scrolls, and perches in just the right spot to ensure Eldrin trips over him at least once a day.

While he is an absolute nuisance to Eldrin, he is fiercely protective of the shop. He has been known to send would-be thieves running in terror with whispered threats that seem to come from the walls themselves. Those who try to cheat Eldrin often find their belongings inexplicably missing, or worse, their memories of the shop foggy and jumbled.

Nyx has an ongoing rivalry with Sniv the Unlucky, whom he sees as a kindred spirit of chaos but refuses to acknowledge as an equal. Their battles of wit often involve outlandish bets, illusion magic, and general mayhem—usually leaving Eldrin with a headache and a mess to clean up. He enjoys taunting Selene Varro’s spies, whispering false secrets just to see if they take the bait.

Despite his pranks, Nyx is deeply loyal to Eldrin. If anyone were to threaten the elf, they would quickly discover that the playful familiar is capable of much, much worse than simple mischief.
