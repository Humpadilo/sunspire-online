# Old Man Griggs

"Old Man Griggs" is a mystery in elven skin, a weathered elf who claims to remember things no living man should. With wild, unkempt silver hair, and pale, milky eyes that see far too much, he is often dismissed as a drunken madman by those who pass him on the streets. But those who listen—really listen—know his words carry fragments of forgotten truths.

Griggs spends most of his time wandering between taverns, the marketplace, and the outskirts of the Arcane Sanctum, muttering about "the past that never was" and "the future that was stolen." He speaks in riddles, often predicting events before they happen, but never in a way that can be easily understood.

Despite his disheveled appearance, there is something unnerving about Griggs. He avoids Archmage Vaelios, who seems to know more about him than he lets on, and Selene Varro has been seen watching him from the shadows more than once. Some say he was once a great scholar, while others believe he is something not entirely mortal, trapped in a body that refuses to age properly. Whatever the truth, one thing is certain—Old Man Griggs knows things no one should know, and those who listen too closely may not like what they hear.
