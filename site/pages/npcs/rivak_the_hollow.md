# Rivak the Hollow

Rivak the Hollow is a whisper of a man, his deep violet skin and curling black horns giving him an eerie, almost unnatural presence. His golden eyes hold no warmth, and his voice is always calm, controlled, and laced with carefully measured words. As the Iron Oath’s primary supplier of rare and exotic materials, he deals in metals not easily found—some legally acquired, others... less so.

Dain Emberforge tolerates him because his goods are the best, but there’s no trust between them. Garrek Steelson outright despises him, seeing him as a crook who hides behind civility. Vella Dornfire, meanwhile, watches him like a hawk, making it painfully clear she’s waiting for the day he slips up. Rivak, however, is never rattled. He finds their suspicions amusing, perhaps even flattering, and always ensures that his dealings are just clean enough to avoid trouble.

Though he keeps out of politics, Rivak has connections everywhere. He’s known to have dealings with Captain Rhogar Skyscale, smuggling certain materials via skyship, and he’s been seen speaking quietly with Selene Varro’s operatives more than once. What they discuss, only they know—but whatever it is, it’s worth keeping secret.

## Notes
A shadowy figure who supplies rare materials for the armory's weapons.
