# Roland Blackstone

“Ashreach burned. My brothers vanished. I’m the only one left to remember.”
Twenty years ago, Roland fought in the Infernal War, stationed at Ashreach Hill, a strategic outpost near the Deadlands. His company was wiped out during a brutal attack by a demonic entity whispered to be The Towerheart.
When Roland awoke, everyone was gone—no bodies, no blood, just silence. The only remnant was a cracked warhorn—his best friend's.
Though the war ended, Roland never found peace. He believes The Towerheart escaped and is still active. For two decades, he's hunted leads, deciphered infernal symbols, and trained relentlessly to end what was started on that hill.
