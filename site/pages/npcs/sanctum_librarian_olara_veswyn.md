# Sanctum Librarian Olara Veswyn

Olara Veswyn is the iron gatekeeper of the Arcane Sanctum’s vast archives, a woman whose presence commands obedience without raising her voice. Her dark brown hair is streaked with silver and always pulled into a severe bun, her keen gray eyes scanning every visitor for intent before granting them access to the tomes under her care. She enforces the sanctum’s rules with unwavering precision, ensuring that forbidden knowledge remains untouched by unworthy hands. Apprentices fear her, scholars respect her, and those who attempt to circumvent her authority often find themselves escorted out by unseen magical forces. Though she serves under Archmage Vaelios, she does not blindly follow him, challenging his decisions when she believes them reckless. She has a cautious but functional relationship with Selene Varro, occasionally granting her limited access to restricted texts in exchange for useful intelligence. She deeply respects Royal Archivist Edric Kael, considering him one of the few individuals who understands the weight of knowledge and its consequences. However, she has grown increasingly wary of Rivak the Hollow, suspecting the enigmatic supplier deals in magical artifacts of dangerous origin. While she holds no personal grudge against Nyx, she has banned the mischievous familiar from the archives, having grown tired of discovering books rearranged by unseen hands.

## Notes
A no-nonsense librarian who maintains order in the Sanctum’s vast archives.
