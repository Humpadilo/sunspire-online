# Sergeant Braska Holt

A half-orc female, Sergeant Braska Holt is the enforcer of discipline within the ranks, the one who turns recruits into warriors through sweat, blood, and sheer willpower. With deep green skin, piercing amber eyes, and a heavily muscled build, she commands respect simply by existing. Her voice is sharp as a blade, her commands are non-negotiable, and her ability to break the strongest-willed recruit is legendary.

Braska admires Thornvale but believes he’s too rigid. “War ain’t a rulebook,” she often tells him. She has a long-standing friendship with Wilfred "Willy" Greaves, often groaning at his antics but secretly appreciating his ability to get results his way. Malrik Dunholm, on the other hand, is everything she despises—a man who sees power as a tool for cruelty instead of protection.

Despite her rough demeanor, Braska cares deeply for her soldiers—not in words, but in actions. She’s the first to patch up a wounded recruit, the first to pull a soldier out of the mud, and the last to leave the field.
