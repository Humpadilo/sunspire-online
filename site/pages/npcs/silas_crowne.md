# Silas Crowne

Silas Crowne is a smooth-talking human rogue who deals in deception like a gambler deals cards—effortlessly and always with a winning hand. A master of disguise and social maneuvering, Silas is known to work with multiple factions, often playing them against each other for personal gain. He has deep ties to Elyssa Dorne, sometimes acting as her eyes and ears in the slums, though neither fully trusts the other. His relationship with Durnik is one of mutual exploitation—Silas gets information, Durnik gets paid. His casual charm hides a sharp mind, one that’s always calculating the risks and rewards of betrayal. Selene Varro keeps a wary eye on him, suspecting he’s involved in something bigger than just backroom deals and stolen coin.
