# Sir Roderic Vaughn

A human male, Sir Roderic Vaughn is discipline, duty, and steel given form. Tall, broad-shouldered, with graying blonde hair and a perpetual scowl, he exudes the unwavering authority of a knight devoted to the faith.

Roderic believes in order above all else—laws exist for a reason, and those who break them must face judgment. He sees Alden as naïve, blinded by soft-hearted hope, and finds Myra reckless, her willingness to bend rules bordering on dangerous. Despite this, he respects Cassiel without question, seeing him as the only leader capable of keeping the faith strong.

Though he seems cold, Roderic is not without a heart. He sees Alden’s unshaken belief and secretly envies it, knowing he lost his own long ago. Myra gets under his skin, but he cannot deny that her skills save as many as his sword does.

Once his loyalty is earned, it is unbreakable—but betray him, and there is no redemption.

## Notes
A knight who believes in absolute order and justice, valuing laws above all else.
