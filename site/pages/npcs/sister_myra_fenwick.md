# Sister Myra Fenwick

A human female, Sister Myra Fenwick is a healer first, a priest second, and a problem solver always. With cropped dark hair, piercing green eyes, and a tone sharper than any blade, she has little patience for nonsense, self-pity, or grand speeches that accomplish nothing.

While she supports Alden’s compassion, she knows kindness alone won’t save the world. She and Sir Roderic Vaughn butt heads constantly, as she sees him as too rigid, too quick to draw steel when words would do. Roderic, in turn, finds her lack of deference infuriating. She is, however, one of the few people who can openly challenge Father Cassiel without immediate consequence, and while she respects him, she isn’t afraid to call out hypocrisy when she sees it.

For all her sharp edges, her hands are steady and warm when she tends to the wounded. She does what must be done—even if it means bending rules to do it.

## Notes
A sharp-tongued priestess and healer, known for her no-nonsense attitude.
