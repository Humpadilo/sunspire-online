# Sniv the Unlucky

Sniv the Unlucky is a goblin who seems to live in a permanent state of “about to be caught.”  
He’s clever, fast-talking, and outrageously confident for someone with a track record of disasters.  

To the public eye, Sniv is a gambler and small-time scam artist who drifts between Eldenhold’s rougher districts and the docks, always chasing easy coin and bigger risks. His luck is catastrophically bad, but his survival instinct is worse.  

Sniv is the kind of person who:
- Owes money to the wrong people
- Wins just enough to stay addicted to the game
- Loses hard enough to need a new identity by morning

Despite that, he’s weirdly likable.  
If the party needs something illegal, someone who knows the criminal underbelly, or a walking plot grenade, Sniv is the goblin you trip over.

**Public-safe notes:**
- Known for gambling rings, shady deals, and being chased.
- Not malicious, just reckless and opportunistic.
- Somehow still alive.
