# Thragar Ironfist

## Notes
Stoic and blunt, Thragar values action over words and has little patience for indecision. Beneath his gruff exterior lies a deep sense of loyalty and honor.

## Appearance
A stocky, broad-shouldered dwarf with a thick, fiery red beard and piercing gray eyes. His intricately crafted plate armor glows faintly with divine energy.

## Class
Forge Cleric / Fighter (Battle Master)
