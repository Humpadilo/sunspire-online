# Valen Silverpalm

A wanderer by birth, Valen Silverpalm was born to an elven mother and human father who were nomadic traders. From his youngest days, Valen has traveled all the kingdoms of SunSpire.
   Valen is what he appears. He is a man of fairness and even temper who is dedicated to protecting those who cannot protect themselves. Through his travels, he has helped many people and developed many skills.  As a young man, he became an accomplished carpenter as he learned to repair the wagons, rafts, and boats used by his family to move goods across the lands. In more recent years, he has become a brewer of fine ales, which he often overindulges in.
He was a young merchant when the Infernal Invasion fell upon the land. During those dark days, he witnessed many atrocities and acts of valor. He used his extensive knowledge of the lands of SunSpire and many contacts to help the countless refugees as best he could. Due to his efforts, many families were reunited, and many innocent lives were saved.
After the war, Valen was a changed man. He no longer cared for the life of a wealthy merchant. The battles between the gods, leaving mortals in the middle, had greatly disturbed him. He started to reason there must be one unifying power more powerful than the gods. After all, something had to have created the gods. Valen set out to discover this power and use it to end the gods' tyranny over man.
Valen has taken up the life of a lone traveling monk. He speaks to any who will listen of the one true power. He uses his many skills, and sometimes his abilities with the staff, to help those in need. But ever at the back of his mind is his quest to free the people of the will of the gods.
