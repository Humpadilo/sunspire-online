# Vella Dornfire

Vella Dornfire is the youngest smith at the Iron Oath Armory, but what she lacks in years, she makes up for with raw talent and fiery determination. Tall and lean, with auburn hair always tied back in a loose braid, she moves around the forge with relentless energy. Her work is sharp, bold, and experimental—traits that both infuriate and impress Dain Emberforge, who constantly scolds her to "slow the hell down" before she burns her damn hands off.

Her rivalry with Garrek Steelson is legendary, the two constantly competing to craft the best weapons, though it’s never truly bitter. She respects his skill and knowledge, even if she thinks he’s too stuck in his ways. She, however, despises Rivak the Hollow. While the others tolerate the tiefling’s dealings, Vella refuses to trust a man who never answers a question directly. She keeps an eye on him whenever he comes to deliver goods, just waiting for the day she catches him in a lie she can use against him.

Despite her rebellious streak, Vella dreams of forging something legendary—something that will make her name last longer than any blade she crafts. She doesn’t care much for politics, but she’s noticed that Selene Varro’s agents have started taking an interest in the armory’s shipments, and that worries her more than she lets on.

## Notes
A young and talented smith, always pushing the limits of weapon forging.
