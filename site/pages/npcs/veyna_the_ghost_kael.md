# Veyna "The Ghost" Kael

Veyna "The Ghost" Kael is a drow assassin whose name is whispered with fear in Valeris’ underworld. She moves unseen, striking targets with precision and disappearing before anyone realizes she was even there. Her motives remain elusive, though it’s believed she works for an unknown benefactor outside the city. Durnik allows her to operate in The Broken Dagger, but only under strict terms—no business within his walls, no exceptions. Rat Fenwick avoids her at all costs, convinced he’s next on her list, while Silas Crowne has tried (and failed) to pry into her past. The most curious relationship she has is with Kaela Voss, the bounty hunter who once tracked her but let her go. There is an uneasy respect between them, though whether it will lead to another confrontation remains to be seen.

## Notes
A deadly and silent assassin, feared by the underworld of Valeris.
