# Wilfred "Willy" Greaves

A halfling male, Wilfred "Willy" Greaves is a scout, a spy, and a walking headache for every commanding officer above him. Short and wiry, with unkempt sandy hair, a perpetual smirk, and a devil-may-care attitude, Willy excels at what he does—gathering intelligence, infiltrating enemy lines, and escaping when no one else can.

Willy drives General Thornvale insane, often referring to him as "old man" just to see his jaw twitch. Sergeant Braska Holt, however, lets him get away with more than she should, knowing that his methods, though unconventional, have saved lives. His relationship with Malrik Dunholm is outright hostile—Willy mockingly calls him "The Dungeon King," and Malrik has nearly thrown him into a cell more than once.

Though Willy laughs at rules and mocks authority, he isn’t reckless—just pragmatic. “You fight when you have to, you run when you need to, and you steal the enemy’s dinner while you’re at it.”

## Notes
A cheeky and unconventional scout, gathering intelligence with unorthodox methods.
